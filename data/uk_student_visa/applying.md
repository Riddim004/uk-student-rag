# When and how to apply

> ⚠️ Illustrative sample data for a software demo. **Not immigration advice.**
> Always verify with the official guidance at https://www.gov.uk/student-visa.

You can only apply once you have a CAS from your sponsor. Applying from outside
the UK, you can usually submit your application some months before your course
starts (commonly up to six months before). If you are already in the UK and
switching or extending, you can typically apply up to three months before the
course begins.

As part of applying you complete the online form, pay the application fee and the
Immigration Health Surcharge, and prove your identity — either at a visa
application centre giving biometrics, or using the smartphone app to scan your
identity document.

Decision times vary. Applications made from outside the UK commonly receive a
decision within about three weeks, while applications from inside the UK may take
around eight weeks. A priority or super-priority service may be available for an
extra fee to get a faster decision.
