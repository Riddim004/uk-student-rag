# Bringing dependants

> ⚠️ Illustrative sample data for a software demo. **Not immigration advice.**
> Always verify with the official guidance at https://www.gov.uk/student-visa.

A dependant is your partner or child. Only some students can bring dependants to
the UK on a Student visa. Eligibility is generally limited — for example to those
sponsored for a full-time postgraduate course at degree level or above of a
qualifying length, or government-sponsored students on a course of a qualifying
length.

Each dependant makes a separate application, pays the application fee and the
Immigration Health Surcharge, and must usually show additional maintenance funds
on top of the student's own funds. A partner must show the relationship is
genuine and subsisting.

Dependants can usually work in the UK, though the same restricted-work rules on
certain roles may apply. A child must normally be supported and cared for
without relying on public funds.
