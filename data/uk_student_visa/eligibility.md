# Student visa eligibility and English language

> ⚠️ Illustrative sample data for a software demo. **Not immigration advice.**
> Always verify with the official guidance at https://www.gov.uk/student-visa.

To apply for a UK Student visa you must have been offered a place on a course by
a licensed student sponsor and hold a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies
(CAS) reference number from that sponsor. You must be 16 or over.

You must be able to speak, read, write and understand English. Applicants
normally prove their knowledge of English by passing an approved Secure English
Language Test (SELT) at the required level, unless they are exempt — for example
nationals of certain majority English-speaking countries, or those who have
already completed a qualifying degree taught in English.

Your sponsor will assess your English before issuing the CAS. Degree-level
students at a higher education provider with a track record of compliance may
have their English assessed by the institution rather than through a SELT.
