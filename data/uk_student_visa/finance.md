# Financial requirement and maintenance funds

> ⚠️ Illustrative sample data for a software demo. **Not immigration advice.**
> Always verify with the official guidance at https://www.gov.uk/student-visa.

As part of a Student visa application you must show that you have enough money to
pay your course fees and to support yourself (maintenance) while studying. The
maintenance amount depends on whether you will be studying in London or outside
London, and is set as a monthly figure for a capped number of months.

The money must usually have been held for a continuous period (commonly 28 days)
before you apply, and the closing balance on your evidence must not drop below
the required amount during that period. Acceptable evidence includes bank
statements, or an official financial sponsorship letter if a government or
approved body is funding you.

If you have been in the UK with valid permission for a qualifying period, you may
be exempt from proving maintenance under the differentiation arrangements.
