# Graduate Route: staying after you finish

> ⚠️ Illustrative sample data for a software demo. **Not immigration advice.**
> Always verify with the official guidance at https://www.gov.uk/graduate-visa.

The Graduate Route lets eligible students stay in the UK to work, or look for
work, after successfully completing an eligible course. You must be in the UK and
hold a valid Student visa at the time you apply, and your sponsor must have told
the Home Office that you completed your course.

The length of permission granted depends on your level of study. Those who
completed an undergraduate or master's degree are typically granted a period of
two years, while those who completed a PhD or other doctoral qualification are
typically granted three years. The Graduate Route cannot be extended.

You do not need a job offer to apply, there is no minimum salary, and you can
work in most jobs, including self-employment. Time on the Graduate Route does not
count toward settlement, but you can switch into a work route such as the Skilled
Worker visa if you meet its requirements.
