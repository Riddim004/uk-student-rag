# Working during your studies

> ⚠️ Illustrative sample data for a software demo. **Not immigration advice.**
> Always verify with the official guidance at https://www.gov.uk/student-visa.

Whether you can work, and how many hours, depends on your course level and your
sponsor. Many students studying a full-time degree-level course at a higher
education provider may work a limited number of hours per week during term time
— commonly up to 20 hours per week — and full time outside term time, such as
during official holidays.

Term-time working hours are a strict weekly limit. You cannot average the hours
across several weeks, and exceeding the limit is a breach of your visa
conditions. Some work is not permitted on a Student visa, including being
self-employed, engaging in business activity, or working as a professional
sportsperson or entertainer.

Your working rights are printed on your visa or set out in your decision. If in
doubt, check what your specific conditions say before accepting any job.
