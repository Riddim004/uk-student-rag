    Why this answer
    How the desk reads

    Questions are embedded locally and matched against 167 chunks of gov.uk guidance. BM25 and dense results are fused by rank; a multilingual cross-encoder then decides whether the file can answer at all.

    What you see

    The answer, the exact passages behind each citation, dense/sparse/rerank scores, the gate verdict, and stage timings. If the evidence is weak, the desk refuses instead of guessing.

    How to check it

    The score strip comes from a 50-question eval with 25 held-out test queries and 8 unanswerable ones. Reports, calibration, incidents and benchmark commands live in the repository.
